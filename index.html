Domains, hosting & ongoing costs

I use GitHub to manage website code and Vercel to publish websites. A custom domain is purchased separately through a domain registrar and connected to your Vercel website. Your domain is your website’s address, like yourbusiness.com. Budget approximately $10–$20+ per year for a standard .com domain as a general estimate, not a fixed registrar quote. The exact registration and renewal prices depend on the chosen name, extension, provider, and current rates; premium domains may cost much more. I’ll confirm the actual price before purchase. You can use a domain you already own, or I can help you choose and set up a new one. You are responsible for ongoing provider charges. Domains are typically renewed yearly, or prepaid for multiple years. Hosting and paid services may be billed monthly, yearly, or for a longer term depending on your chosen plan. These recurring charges are separate from my website design fee and domain setup service fee. I also charge a separate service fee to register, connect, or set up a domain for you. The domain should be registered in your name or business account so you keep ownership. We’ll agree on all costs before I begin.

Introductory project pricing in USD. Starter: $150. Business: $300. Premium: from $500. Final pricing depends on scope. New requests or changes outside the original agreed scope cost extra. You’ll receive the additional price for approval before that work begins. Domain registration fees, my domain setup service, research and content planning, hosting, paid services, and ongoing maintenance are quoted separately unless included in your written quote. All extra charges are agreed on before work begins.

A few good questions

Before we begin.

What do I need to get started?

Your business name, services, contact details, and any existing logo, photos, or website. If you’re still gathering these, we can discuss what your project needs first.

How long will my website take?

The timeline depends on the number of pages, features, and how quickly content is ready. You’ll receive a project timeline with your quote before work begins.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. Share your current website and what you’d like to improve. We’ll review the layout, mobile experience, content, and next steps together.

Are domain and hosting costs included?

I use GitHub for website code and Vercel for deployment. Custom domains are registered separately and connected to your site. Domain registration fees are separate from website design. You pay the provider’s domain renewal fees, typically yearly or for a multi-year term. Hosting and other paid services may be billed monthly, yearly, or for a longer term depending on the plan. A price advertised per month may require payment for the full term upfront. Any introductory offer may renew at a different rate. If you want me to find, register, connect, or set up a domain for you, I charge an additional service fee. Any paid hosting or third-party services are also separate unless your written quote includes them. I’ll confirm all costs with you before proceeding.

Can you research and plan my website for me?

Yes. If you want me to research your business, industry, competitors, or content to develop your website’s design or copy, that research and planning costs extra unless it is included in your written quote. I’ll agree on the research scope and additional price with you before starting.

What if I request changes or add something later?

Revisions included in your original project agreement will be listed in your quote. New requests, features, pages, or changes you did not mention and agree on before the project began cost extra. I’ll explain the additional price and get your approval before doing that work. Ongoing maintenance is quoted separately.
